Being busy at work for a long time, my return to hiking felt like a quiet revelation. After nearly half a year away from the trails, I embarked on a journey to Myōjin Gatake.

Despite being easily accessible from Yokohama, I found not many people around, making the experience all the more intimate and enjoyable.
