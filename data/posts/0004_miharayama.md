One of the most interesting hikes in Japan so far! There is a midnight ferry connection from Yokohama (starting from Tokyo) arriving in the morning just to see the sunrise above Ōshima. You can find more information on [Tōkaisen homepage](https://www.tokaikisen.co.jp/schedule/#Oshima).

Depending on the weather situation the ships will use either Motomachi port (元町港) or Okata port (岡田港) and it changes every day. Be aware that the bus stops and departure times will change dependending on the port which is used on that day. You can find the currently used port on the homepage and it's usually displayed in all buses on Ōshima.

## Hike info

🕐 05:10
📍 10.6 km
⬆️ 500 m
⬇️ 440 m

![Hike route](/0004_miharayama/route.jpeg)

### Access

#### Start

1. Ōsanbashi terminal (大さん橋ターミナル)
2. ↓ Ferry ↓
3. Port Motomachi / Port Okata  (元町港・岡田港)
4. ↓ Bus ↓
5. Mt. Mihara onsen (三原山温泉)

#### Finish

1. Mt. Mihara summit entrance area (三原山頂口)
2. ↓ Bus ↓
3. Port Motomachi / Port Okata  (元町港・岡田港)
4. ↓ Ferry ↓
5. Ōsanbashi terminal (大さん橋ターミナル)
